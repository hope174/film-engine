THE FILM ENGINE

This folder turns a written script into an audio film. You write who says what,
and which sounds and music play, in a plain text file. The engine gives every
character a voice, adds the sounds and music, and saves the finished film as
an MP3 file you can listen to or share.


THE FIRST TIME

Double-click Setup.bat and wait until it says "All done". You only need to do
this once on each computer.


THE EASY WAY: FILM MAKER

Double-click Film Maker.bat. A window opens where you can do everything:
write the script, add characters, lines and sounds, make the film and listen to it.
It works with screen readers such as NVDA and JAWS.

The window has three parts. Press Tab to move between them, or use these keys:
Alt L goes to the film list. Arrow up and down to pick a film, and its script opens.
Alt S goes to the script, where you can read and type.
Alt G goes to the messages, which say what has happened.

The keys you will use most:
Control N starts a new film.
Control K adds a character to the cast and lets you choose and hear their voice.
Control L adds a spoken line. Control B adds a sound or music.
Control P adds a pause. Control T stops sounds. Control E marks a new scene.
Control S saves the script.
F7 checks the script for mistakes. If there is one, it tells you, and takes you to that line.
F5 makes the film. When it is ready, it asks if you want to listen.
F9 plays the finished film.
Control F finds words. Control G goes to a line number.
F1 opens the syntax examples. The Help menu lists every shortcut.

New lines are added below the line you are on, so move to the right place first.
All of these are also in the menus at the top. Press Alt to reach them.


MAKING A FILM WITHOUT THE WINDOW

1. Double-click New film.bat and type a name for your film, such as the haunted house.
   A new folder is made inside the films folder, and its script opens in Notepad.
2. Write your script and save it. The file "Syntax.txt" shows how every kind of line
   should look, with right and wrong examples. "Script guide.txt" explains everything
   in more detail. The starter script shows the basics.
3. Double-click Make a film.bat and type the film's folder name, such as the-haunted-house.
   The engine reads the script, makes the voices and mixes everything together.
4. When it says "Done", type y and press Enter to listen to it.

The finished film is saved in your film's folder, inside a folder called output.
Next to it is a timeline file that lists what happens at each minute and second.


THE OTHER BUTTONS

Check a script.bat looks for mistakes in a script without making the film. It is quick.
List voices.bat shows all the voices you can give your characters, in Notepad.
Try a voice.bat lets you hear a voice saying anything you like, in any style.
List sounds.bat lists the sounds you can use without any sound files, such as rain and thunder.
List words.bat shows every word you can put in brackets, such as whisper, echo or behind, in Notepad.
Save my work.bat saves changes to the engine and the shared sounds folder in the history.
Your films are not part of that history. They stay private, on this computer,
and OneDrive keeps a copy of them.


WHERE THINGS GO

films: one folder for each film. Each has script.txt, and a sounds folder for that film's sound files.
sounds: sound and music files that every film can use.
sound-folders.txt: list other folders on the computer that have sounds or music in them,
such as a music library, and the engine will look there too.


WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG

If the script has a mistake, the engine tells you the line number and what is wrong,
and often suggests the right word. Fix the line, save, and try again.

Voices come from Microsoft over the internet, so making a film needs the internet.
Lines that have been made before are saved, so making the film again is quick and
only fetches the lines you changed.

If the engine says "Something went wrong inside the engine", that is not your fault.
Send the file it names to the person who looks after the engine.


ASKING AN AI TO MAKE A FILM

An AI helper, such as Claude, can write and make films in this folder too. It reads
AGENTS.md to learn how. Just ask it something like: make a short film about a lost dog
who finds his way home. The film appears in the films folder like any other, and you
can change its script yourself afterwards.
